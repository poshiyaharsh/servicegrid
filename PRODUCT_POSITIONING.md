# Product Positioning — ServiceGrid

**Phase:** 01 — Research and Product Discovery  
**Date:** 2026-11-26

---

## 1. Positioning Statement
For **growing field service businesses in India and emerging/global markets** (AC, appliance, RO, CCTV, solar, electrical, plumbing, and facility maintenance) that struggle with fragmented WhatsApp/Excel workflows, unreliable offline mobile apps, and expensive or rigid enterprise tools, **ServiceGrid** is the **all-in-one Field Service Operations SaaS** that connects offline-first technician execution, WhatsApp-first customer communication, auditable inventory, AMC management, and GST-ready billing in one intuitive platform.

---

## 2. Core Differentiators

1. **Offline-First Technician Experience:** Built on SQLite + idempotent outbox sync so technicians never lose job data, photos, or signatures in basements or low-signal zones.
2. **15-Minute SMB Onboarding:** Pre-configured trade templates (AC, RO, CCTV, Solar, Plumbing/Electrical) without requiring enterprise consultants.
3. **Complete Service-to-Cash Workflow:** Request → Dispatch → Field Execution → Parts Deduction → Estimate/Invoice → UPI/Online Payment → Accounting Export.
4. **WhatsApp-First Communication:** Official WhatsApp Business Platform adapters for booking confirmations, technician ETA, estimate approvals, invoices, and AMC reminders.
5. **India-Ready Financial Core, Global-Ready Architecture:** Native GSTIN, HSN/SAC, CGST/SGST/IGST, and UPI alongside multi-currency, multi-timezone, and configurable tax/fiscal rules.
6. **AMC & Asset Lifecycle Built In:** Not bolted on—tracks equipment serials, warranties, preventive schedules, and contract profitability.
7. **Pragmatic, Safe AI:** Accelerates triage and reporting without ever making autonomous financial or job-closure decisions.

---

## 3. Competitive Wedge
- **vs. WhatsApp + Excel + Tally:** Eliminates lost leads, unbilled spare parts, and manual invoice re-entry.
- **vs. ServiceTitan / Dynamics 365:** 10x faster to deploy and priced for SMB-to-mid-market economics without sacrificing offline reliability or tenant security.
- **vs. FieldWeb / Zoho FSM:** Adds true offline-first Android sync, deep AMC + serial/warehouse ledger, and native India GST/UPI workflows.
- **vs. FieldEZ / Dista:** Modern, uncluttered UI with low-battery offline architecture and transparent self-serve onboarding.
