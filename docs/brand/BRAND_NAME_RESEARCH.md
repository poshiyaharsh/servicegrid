# Brand Name Research & Evaluation — FieldSutra (Working Name: ServiceGrid)

**Phase:** 02A — Product Name Research  
**Date:** 2026-11-26  
**Note on Verification:** Web search was used to screen for obvious existing SaaS/software conflicts. Formal domain registrar checks (`.com`, `.in`, `.io`) and trademark registry searches (IP India, USPTO, EUIPO) **have not been executed** and must be completed before legal incorporation or domain purchase.

---

## 1. Evaluation Criteria (1–5 Scale, 5 = Best)

1. **Memorability (MEM):** Easy to recall after hearing once.
2. **Pronunciation (PRO):** Unambiguous in Indian English and global English.
3. **Uniqueness (UNQ):** Stands out from generic "Field*" / "Service*" clones.
4. **Professional Appearance (PRF):** Credible for B2B SaaS buyers and enterprise facility managers.
5. **International Usability (INT):** Works globally without awkward cultural meaning.
6. **India-Market Suitability (IND):** Resonates with Indian SMB owners, dispatchers, and technicians.
7. **Domain Outlook (DOM):** Likely availability of `.com` / `.in` / `.app` (preliminary heuristic; requires registrar verification).
8. **Trademark Risk (TMR):** Inverse risk score (5 = lowest apparent conflict in preliminary web search).
9. **Software Conflict (SWC):** Inverse conflict score (5 = no active competing FSM SaaS found in web search).

## 3. Final Brand Recommendation: **FieldSutra**

### Why **FieldSutra** is the Strongest Candidate
1. **Meaning & Positioning:** "Field" grounds the product immediately in field service operations, while "Sutra" (thread/formula/system) conveys connecting every thread of the business—customers, technicians, inventory, AMC contracts, and GST/global billing—into one cohesive operational system.
2. **India-First, Global-Ready:** Instantly memorable and culturally resonant in India without sounding informal, while remaining crisp, phonetic, and authoritative for global expansion.
3. **Cleanest Competitive Landscape:** Unlike `FieldGrid`, `Fieldora`, `KaryaFlow`, `Fixora`, `FieldLedger`, and `ServiceGrid`, preliminary web searches did not surface an active competing FSM SaaS using **FieldSutra**.
4. **Monorepo Continuity:** The repository and internal package namespace (`servicegrid` / `@servicegrid/*`) can remain stable while **FieldSutra** serves as the commercial product brand.

### Required Next Legal Steps (Before Public Launch)
- Verify domain availability (`fieldsutra.com`, `fieldsutra.in`, `fieldsutra.io`).
- Conduct formal trademark clearance in Class 9 & Class 42 (IP India, USPTO, WIPO).
- Verify social handle availability (`@fieldsutra`).
